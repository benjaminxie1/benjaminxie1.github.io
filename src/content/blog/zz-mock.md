About four months ago, my good friend Dhilen suggested we participate in a hackathon none of us had any business entering. We had never worked in a wet lab, and we didn’t know what a digital twin was supposed to look like.

So we built one anyway—a world model of a wet lab that lets you run an experiment before you run the experiment. This is the story of how a bad idea at 2 a.m. turned into *LabTwin*, and what I learned about scope, sleep, and saying no to good ideas.
