This site is new. The landing page is a willow over a pond, drawn entirely in ASCII on a canvas: the strands sway with a slow breeze (move your mouse to push them around), leaves fall and float, and clicking the water makes ripples.

The tagline is from the Song dynasty poet Lu You: *柳暗花明又一村*, roughly "past the dark willows and bright flowers, another village." It's usually read as: when the road seems to end, keep walking.

Under the hood it's [Astro](https://astro.build). Posts like this one are just Markdown files in `src/content/blog/`, and the whole thing deploys to GitHub Pages on every push.
